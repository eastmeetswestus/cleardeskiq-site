Does your small business run on quotes, hours and money?

Most small businesses run on the same four things. If yours does, ClearDesk IQ can run it today. Some screens use contractor words, such as job or subcontractor.

Quotes

Price a piece of work from your costs, add overhead and a target margin, and send a clean PDF quote.

Hours

Record staff hours with regular and overtime rates. Approved hours become labor cost on the work they belong to.

Money

Invoices and payments, expenses with receipts, money owed by age, and a 13-week cash plan.

Profit

See profit and margin for each piece of work, and let Profit review flag what is slipping.

A good fit if you…

  • quote work before you do it
  • pay staff by the hour
  • send invoices and chase payments
  • want to know which work actually makes money
  • prefer one payment to a monthly bill
  • want your records on your own computer

Not the right tool if you need…

  • a cash register or point-of-sale system
  • inventory or stock tracking
  • payroll, tax filing or a full accounting ledger
  • a Mac, phone or web app
