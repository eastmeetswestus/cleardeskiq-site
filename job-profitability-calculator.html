How the numbers work

  • Labor is hours times your labor cost per hour.
  • Direct costs are labor, materials, subcontractors and other job costs.
  • Overhead is your overhead percentage of direct costs.
  • Profit is the price minus total cost. Margin is profit divided by price.
  • Price for a target margin is total cost divided by (1 minus the target margin).

Worked example. Price $18,500. Costs: labor $3,300 (60 hours at $55), materials $7,200, subcontractors $4,200, other $350, and overhead $1,505 (10% of direct costs). Total cost is $16,555, so profit is $1,945 and margin is 10.5%. The price for a 20% target margin is $16,555 divided by 0.80, which is $20,693.75, rounded up to $20,694.

Underpriced means margin under 10% or a loss. Thin means at least 10% but under your target. Healthy means at or above your target.

Frequently asked questions

What is job margin?

Job margin is profit divided by the price you charge. A $18,500 job that keeps $1,945 has a 10.5% job margin.

What is the difference between job profit and job margin?

Job profit is dollars: the price minus total cost. Job margin is the share of the price you keep. Two jobs can make the same profit and have very different margins.

How is overhead counted?

Overhead here is a percentage of direct costs (labor, materials, subcontractors and other job costs). Use the percentage your business applies to job costs.

What is the price for a target margin?

Total cost divided by (1 minus the target margin). For a total cost of $16,555 and a 20% target, the price is $20,694.

What do Underpriced, Thin and Healthy mean?

Underpriced means a margin under 10% or a loss. Thin means at least 10% but under your target. Healthy means at or above your target.

What if I do not know my labor cost per hour?

Use your hourly rate from the contractor hourly rate calculator, which covers owner pay, tax, overhead and billable hours.

Does the calculator work for a job I have already finished?

Yes. Enter the final price and the actual costs to see what the job kept. For a bid versus finished-job comparison, use the estimate vs actual job cost calculator.

Are the numbers I type stored or sent?

No. The page runs in your browser. Nothing you type is stored or sent.
