Transfer a complete backup file. In 0.4.2 and later it includes attached photos and receipts. Excel exports and program folders are not the live workspace.

Steps

  1. Save a complete backup (Reports & exports → Save backup) and preserve pending work before changing computers or versions. In 0.4.2 and later the backup includes attached photos and receipts. An Excel export cannot restore the company.
  2. Updates arrive through the Microsoft Store. Your records and attachments folder stay in place during an update or a reinstall.
  3. For a standalone replacement computer, restore the backup into the intended standalone workspace and compare totals before resuming work. The confirmation shows how many photo/receipt files the backup holds.
  4. Backups made by version 0.4.1 or earlier hold records only. With one of those, close the app on both computers and also copy the attachments folder from the old data folder to the same place on the new computer.
  5. For an additional team computer, use Office & devices and a separate invitation. Do not clone another paired profile or attempt to merge two independent company backups. Photos and receipts stay on the computer where they were added.
  6. Replacing an office host or a paired laptop with pending work requires planned recovery. See the shared-office recovery guide before reconnecting devices.

Check that it worked

Compare key job and invoice balances, open a job or expense with photos, then close and reopen to confirm the records persist.

Still stuck?

If a new release refuses an existing workspace, preserve it and report the version pair. Do not remove backup validation or downgrade over live data.
