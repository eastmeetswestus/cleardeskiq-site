Questions

Questions people ask first

What is ClearDesk IQ?

A Windows app for small contractors. It tracks quotes, jobs, invoices, expenses, crew hours and job profit in one place.

How much does it cost?

$79.99 once, on the Microsoft Store. No monthly fee. The Store shows the final price at checkout.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11 PC, 64-bit.

Does it need an account or internet?

No account to create. It works offline.

Where are my records?

On your own computer. Keep regular backups. Nothing is sent to us.

What does it not do?

No bank feeds, payroll, tax filing, email sending, payment reminders or card payments. The box above lists every limit.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export to Excel or CSV any time.

Does it work on a phone or Mac?

No. ClearDesk IQ is a Windows PC app only.
