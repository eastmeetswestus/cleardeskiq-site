Questions

What do people ask first?

What kind of software is ClearDesk IQ?

Not accounting software: ClearDesk IQ is a complete business management system for small businesses. It runs quotes, jobs, invoices, expenses, employee hours, profit and cash planning. For tax returns, payroll filings and a full ledger, keep using your accountant or payroll service, and give them the accountant-ready CSV export.

How much does it cost?

$79.99 USD, paid once on the Microsoft Store. There are no monthly fees and no subscription. The Store shows the final price, including any tax, at checkout.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11 PC, 64-bit (x64). It does not run on Mac, phones, tablets or Windows on ARM.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed, and the app works offline. You need the internet to buy and download it from the Microsoft Store.

Where are my records stored?

On your own computer, in a records folder inside your Windows user folder. Uninstalling or reinstalling the app does not delete them. The Backup button saves one complete file, including job and expense photos, that you can keep on a USB drive or another folder.
