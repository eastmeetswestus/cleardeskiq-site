Change Order Pricing Calculator

A change order is added or changed work the customer approves after the contract is signed. Price it on direct cost plus overhead, then take profit as a margin on the price. Enter the direct cost of the change, your overhead percentage and the margin you want. Free, runs in your browser, no sign-up.

Formula. Price = (direct cost + overhead) divided by (1 minus the profit margin). Overhead = direct cost times overhead %. Profit = price minus the cost with overhead.

Example. A $3,200 change with 30% overhead and a 20% margin: overhead $960, cost $4,160, price $5,200, profit $1,040. Charging direct cost only would leave $2,000 of overhead and profit unpaid.

How the numbers work

  • Overhead is the direct cost times your overhead percentage.
  • Price = (direct cost + overhead) divided by (1 minus the profit margin). The margin is measured on the price, the same way the job margin is.
  • Charging direct cost only means the price equals the direct cost, so overhead and profit are not paid.
  • Negative inputs count as 0. A margin of 100% or more has no price, so the calculator asks for a lower margin.

What this does not count

  • Sales tax on the change. Charge tax as your contract and local rules require.
  • Schedule impact or other scope changes. Price those separately.
  • Whether the customer approves the change. Your contract sets how changes are approved.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between an estimate and a change order?

An estimate prices the original job before you sign. A change order prices added or changed work after the contract is signed. The same formula can price both.

Why charge overhead and profit on a change?

Overhead is the cost of running the business: insurance, truck, phone and office. Added work adds overhead too. Charging direct cost only leaves that overhead and any profit unpaid.

Is the margin on the price or markup on cost?

This calculator uses margin on the price, the same convention as the job profitability calculator. A 20% margin is a 25% markup on cost. The markup vs margin calculator converts either way.

What if the change is mostly labor?

Enter your labor cost for the time as direct cost. The contractor hourly rate calculator shows how to set the labor cost per hour.

What do I enter for overhead?

Use the overhead percentage you apply to direct cost. The contractor hourly rate calculator shows how yearly overhead changes the rate you need.

Can I use it for a credit, when work is taken out?

This page prices added work only. It does not price savings from removed scope. Agree the credit amount with the customer and record it on the change order.

Does ClearDesk IQ track change orders?

Yes. The Windows app records each change order as Draft, Submitted, Approved or Rejected. Only approved changes adjust the contract value and budget.

Are the numbers I type stored or sent?

No. The page runs in your browser. Nothing you type is stored or sent.
