Understand category overruns and missing baselines

The category comparison uses the original accepted estimate, not an updated detailed budget.

Steps

  1. Open Show calculation & limits on a category flag to compare original estimated cost, recorded cost and the difference.
  2. Recorded cost exceeding a nonzero original category amount triggers a comparison. It is not a second loss to add to the overall job forecast.
  3. Approved changes and estimate overhead can be unallocated. Check whether new scope explains the extra cost.
  4. A category with no original amount is labeled missing a baseline, not assumed to be a zero-cost plan.
  5. A manually created job without an accepted estimate can still use its total cost budget, but cannot establish category overruns.
  6. For future jobs, build the estimate with the relevant materials, labor, equipment and other categories before accepting it.

Check that it worked

You know which baseline is being compared and which scope or costs it leaves out.

Still stuck?

Check the source records before making a financial decision. Keep a backup before corrections. The review cannot find work or spending that has never been entered.
