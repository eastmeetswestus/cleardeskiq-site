Everything included

What does a job look like from bid to paid?

Every feature below is in the app today, for one payment.

Bids that protect your margin

Line items for materials, labor, subs and equipment, with overhead, target margin, tax, deposit and a minimum-profit floor. PDF estimates and follow-up dates.

Job profit, live

An accepted bid becomes the job. Estimate versus actual by category, change orders, and forecast final cost and profit.

Get paid for every stage

Deposit, progress and final invoices by amount or percent complete, retainage, partial payments, and days late on every overdue invoice.

Job costs with receipts

Record each cost against the job or company overhead, paid or unpaid, with the receipt photo attached.

Crew hours and labor cost

Employees with regular and overtime rates. Draft hours change nothing until approved; approved hours land on the job as labor cost.

Bonus: profit-leak detector

Profit review flags losing jobs, jobs under margin, invoices over 30 days late, unbilled extras and missing details.

13-week cash plan

See a low-cash week coming, and test what slow-paying customers would do to it.

Bring your lists, hand off clean files

Import customers or a price list from Excel or CSV. Export to Excel or accountant-ready CSV.

Office PC and job-site laptops

Laptops share records with the office over your own network and keep working offline. A crew role limits what a field laptop can see.
