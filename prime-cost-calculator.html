Prime cost calculator for restaurants

Prime cost is your food and drink cost plus your total labor, as a percent of sales for the same period. Enter the period's numbers to see prime cost and where it stands. Free, runs in your browser, no sign-up.

A free calculator. It is not the Restaurant Edition, which is coming soon.

The period

Digits and one point for decimals. Use one period at a time, such as a week or a month. Rent and utilities are not part of prime cost.

Estimates only, from the numbers you enter. Not tax, legal or accounting advice. Example numbers are fictional.

The formula

  • Prime cost = food and drink cost + total labor.
  • Prime cost % = prime cost divided by sales, times 100.

A worked example

Sales $22,000, food and drink cost $6,000, labor $7,000. Prime cost is $13,000, which is 59.09% of sales. A second period with sales $30,000, food and drink $9,900 and labor $8,400 gives 61.00%.

What to watch

  • Use the same labor definition every period: wages, payroll taxes and benefits.
  • A high percentage in a slow week is normal. Look at the trend across several weeks.
  • Labor is only part of the picture. For dish-level costs, use the food cost calculator.
