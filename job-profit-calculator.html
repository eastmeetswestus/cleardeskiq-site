How the numbers work

  • Direct costs are materials, labor, subcontractors and other job costs.
  • Overhead is your overhead percentage of direct costs.
  • Total cost is direct costs plus overhead. Profit is the price minus total cost.
  • Margin is profit divided by price. It is not the same as markup.
  • Price for a target margin is total cost divided by (1 minus the target margin).

Healthy means at or above your target margin. Thin means at least 10% but under your target. Underpriced means under 10% or a loss.
